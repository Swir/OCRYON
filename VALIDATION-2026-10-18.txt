Defects found and fixed during real Windows qualification:
- quoted pytesseract tessdata arguments failed on Windows;
- a build-host Poppler ICU DLL broke frozen QtCore imports;
- Tesseract narrow path arguments broke OCR in Unicode installation directories;
- upstream --list-langs failed in Unicode working directories;
- several recovered process-test fixtures assumed Unix execution;
- preprocessing controls, accessibility, diagnostics and recovery were incomplete.
